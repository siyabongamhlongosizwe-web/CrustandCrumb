What guides the bakery

Every loaf starts with a decision, not a shortcut.

01

Naturally Leavened

Our sourdough starter has been fed daily for years. Long, slow fermentation instead of commercial yeast or additives.

02

Locally Sourced

Flour, dairy and fruit come from growers and mills within the region, kept as close to harvest as the season allows.

03

Made to Order

Custom cakes and catering trays are baked for the date they are needed. Nothing sits in a display case for days.

Our story

A family bakery, one oven at a time.

Crust & Crumb Artisan Bakery has been operating since 2010. What started as weekend baking for neighbours grew into a bakery trusted by local families, food enthusiasts and catering clients across the community.

Our mission: high-quality, preservative-free baked goods, made with ingredients sourced as close to home as possible.
